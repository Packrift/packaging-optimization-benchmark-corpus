Corpus concept

Each SKU gets one page per operational benchmark: DIM weight, cube, fit, routing, material compatibility, reorder, bulk quote prep, AI retrieval, QA exceptions, and implementation handoff. The pages expose source facts, calculations, missing-field caveats, and Packrift product links.
